# Draft cover letter

Dear Editors of the *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*,

Please consider the manuscript **“Orientation Reliability in Oriented Object Detection: Geometry-Aware Measurement and Image-Level Risk Control”** for publication as a research article.

The manuscript addresses a measurement problem in remote-sensing object detection: conventional detection accuracy does not directly establish whether an oriented bounding box has a trustworthy long-axis angle. The work contributes a controlled full-evaluator validity test, a geometry-aware orientation estimand, risk--coverage diagnostics, image-level finite-sample risk control, and a 600-target independent double-annotation study. Its evidence spans DIOR-R, FAIR1M-v1.0, SODA-A, and a local DOTA-v1.0 train/validation protocol.

The paper is intentionally not presented as a new detector or a state-of-the-art accuracy result. It focuses on evaluation validity and responsible use of orientation outputs in dense aerial imagery. We believe this emphasis fits the journal's interest in rigorous methods for photogrammetry, remote sensing, and geospatial image interpretation.
